Privacy Policy

At ClassMate, your privacy, code security, and learning telemetry are safeguarded by state-of-the-art encryption protocols, strict isolation standards, and zero-compromise data governance.

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential developer information and platform usage metrics to provide seamless, interactive learning sandboxes and mentorship tracking:

  • Account & Enrollment Data: Full name, verified email address, phone number, and student registration credentials when enrolling in our batches.
  • Interactive Sandbox Telemetry: Code submissions, compilation execution outputs, terminal commands, and live lab progress across our online environments.
  • Payment & Transaction Records: Transaction IDs and uploaded proof-of-payment receipts for batch verification. We never store sensitive banking PINs or credit card CVV numbers directly on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data fuels your personalized learning journey, performance benchmarking, and system optimization:

  • Sandbox & Lab Personalization: Maintaining state isolation and real-time execution environments tailored to your current course syllabus and problem assignments.
  • Mentorship & Placement Analytics: Generating real-time skill scorecards and interview readiness reports shared exclusively with verified FAANG mentors and our career cell upon your explicit consent.
  • Platform Security & Anti-Abuse: Monitoring network traffic, rate limits, and API requests to prevent DDoS attempts, account sharing, or unauthorized scraping of proprietary curriculum assets.

3. Data Encryption & Storage Security

We implement strict zero-trust security postures across our entire infrastructure architecture:

All data transmitted between your browser and ClassMate servers is secured via TLS 1.3 encryption with strict Forward Secrecy. Student database records and code repository snapshots are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256-GCM algorithms. Regular vulnerability scans, penetration tests, and automated access reviews ensure unauthorized parties cannot intercept or tamper with your records.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade student personal data or codebase telemetry to third-party data brokers under any circumstances.

Information is only shared under strictly audited agreements with:

  • Infrastructure & Cloud Providers: Secure hosting environments (such as AWS/GCP computing nodes) solely for executing code compilation and hosting video lectures.
  • Legal Compliance Requirements: When compelled by valid judicial subpoenas, court orders, or statutory mandates from authorized regulatory authorities.

5. Your Privacy Rights & Controls

You retain full sovereignty over your personal data on ClassMate. You have the right to request a complete export of your student profile, lab submissions, and telemetry archives at any time. Furthermore, you may request account deletion or data anonymization by contacting our support team at info.sayansarkar@gmail.com or reaching out via our official support hotline at +91 9123025207.
